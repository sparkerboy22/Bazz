Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about booking, paying and staying safe with bruz.

How do I book a ride?

Open the app, enter your destination, choose a ride and confirm. A nearby driver is matched to you within moments and you can follow the trip live on the map.

Is bruz. available 24/7?

Yes. Rides, deliveries and car hire are available around the clock, every day of the year.

What payment methods do you accept?

You can pay by debit or credit card, or with a digital wallet, directly inside the app.

How are drivers vetted?

Every driver completes identity, licence and background checks before they can accept a single trip.

How can I contact customer support?

Use the contact form on this page or reach us through the in-app help centre at any time.
